Nana's Baby Essentials Registry Checklist

Newborn Basics
- Bodysuits and vests
- Sleepsuits and pajamas
- Swaddles and receiving blankets
- Mittens, socks, and caps

Feeding Essentials
- Bottles and bottle brush
- Burp cloths
- Bibs
- Breastfeeding or pumping support items

Diapering
- Newborn diapers
- Wipes
- Diaper cream
- Changing mat or changing station

Sleep Space
- Cot, crib, or bassinet
- Waterproof sheets
- Sleep sacks
- Nursery monitor

Bath and Care
- Baby bath tub
- Hooded towels
- Baby soap and lotion
- Nail care kit

Travel and Outings
- Car seat
- Stroller
- Diaper bag
- Baby carrier

Comfort and Play
- Soft toys
- Activity mat
- Rocker or bouncer
- Soothers and teething support

Registry Notes
- Add the essentials first.
- Include a mix of daily basics and bigger-ticket items.
- Share your registry link early with close family and friends.
- Review your registry regularly and remove duplicates.

Prepared by Nana's Baby Essentials.
